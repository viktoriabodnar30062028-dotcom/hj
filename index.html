FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with the free plan?

Simply click the "Get Started" button and follow the signup process. No credit card is required for the free plan.

Can I upgrade my plan at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time directly from your account dashboard. The change will be applied immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, including Visa, Mastercard and American Express. We also support PayPal.

Is my data safe with your service?

We use modern security practices to help keep your information safe and private. Your trust is our priority.

Do you offer a refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. Contact our support team for assistance.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription from your account settings. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing cycle.
